Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026 · Controller: Nicholas Family LTD, London, United Kingdom · Contact: office@aichatbot.rs

1. Our privacy-first design

SafeNest AI is built so that we collect as little personal data as possible. The app requires no account, no registration and no login. We do not know who you are.

2. What data is processed and where

Stored only on your device (browser localStorage, never sent to us): child profiles (first name and age group you enter), scan history and images, your language choice, detection feedback used for on-device learning, and subscription/trial state.

Processed transiently for analysis: when you scan a room or food, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our analysis function and forwarded to an AI vision provider solely to generate the safety analysis. Images are processed in memory for that single request; we do not build a database of your images and we never use them to identify people.

Voice: speech recognition and speech output run on your device via your browser's built-in speech engine. Your typed or transcribed question (text only) is sent for answering.

Payments: subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.

3. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Processing of scan images is necessary to perform the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)). We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not profile users.

4. Children's data

The app is intended for use by parents and guardians (adults). Child profile details you enter (first name, age group) never leave your device.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR/UK GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Since your data lives on your device, you can exercise most rights directly: deleting the app data (or scans/profiles inside the app) permanently removes it. For anything else, contact office@aichatbot.rs. You may also lodge a complaint with the ICO (UK) or your local supervisory authority.

6. Data retention & security

On-device data remains until you delete it. Transient analysis requests are not retained as a copy by us. All network traffic is encrypted (TLS/HTTPS).

7. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new "last updated" date.
