How it works

Three steps to a safer home

1

📷 Scan

Open the camera and slowly pan around the room — or snap a single photo. A fast AI model runs directly on your phone, and a cloud vision AI double-checks every detail, down to small objects like batteries, screws and cords.

2

💡 Understand

Every hazard is marked on screen with its severity. Tap any marker to see why it's dangerous for your child's exact age, real injury statistics from WHO and CDC sources, and what to do about it.

3

✅ Fix

Follow the step-by-step fix, mark hazards as resolved, and track your home's safety score over time. The AI learns from your feedback and gets more precise with every scan.

FAQ

Questions parents ask us

How does SafeNest AI detect dangers?

A fast AI model runs directly on your phone and marks risky objects in real time, while a cloud vision AI adds detailed explanations, real injury statistics (WHO/CDC sources) and step-by-step fixes — all adapted to your child's age profile.

Is it really free to try?

Yes — your first 7 days are completely free with no card required. After that it's €7/month and you can cancel anytime.

Can it check food for my baby?

Yes. Photograph a meal, ingredient or product label and the AI tells you if it's appropriate for your child's age — allergens, choking risks, honey rules for under-12-months, and safe serving tips.

Which languages are supported?

More than 40 world languages. You choose yours on first launch, and every detection, warning and voice answer follows it.

Is my data private?

SafeNest AI is privacy-first and GDPR compliant: no account, profiles and history stay on your device, and images are processed transiently for the analysis only. See our Privacy Policy.

Does it replace supervision or a doctor?

No. SafeNest AI is an educational aid — it never replaces active adult supervision and it is not medical advice. For health concerns always consult your paediatrician.
